# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best
for the community. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not tolerated.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/miladdhs). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
